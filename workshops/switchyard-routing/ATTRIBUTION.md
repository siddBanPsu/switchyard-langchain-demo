# Data and recording attribution

The checkout screenshot, support incident, logs and runbook are synthetic demonstration fixtures. Their model outputs are recorded responses, not real customer activity. The original incident fallback is synthetic as well.

The Ubuntu examples use the [Ubuntu Chat Logs Corpus](https://www.convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/chatlogs.html), Sarkar et al. (2025), formatted for ConvoKit by Axel Bax, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Original source messages, public-source provenance and the grouped adaptation are included under `data/ubuntu/`. The handover uses conversation 79.2 and preserves all 79 messages. Grouping, tooling and evaluation are adaptations. Historical troubleshooting advice is evidence for summarization, not current installation guidance.

OpenRouter model metadata and price snapshots are retained as dated reference evidence, with source URLs in each snapshot. Inference used a separately configured gateway; estimates do not represent its invoices.

Published notebooks have outputs cleared and paid calls/tracing disabled by default. Raw comparison recordings retain model responses, usage and prompts; private endpoint values and local workspace paths have been redacted where present. Recorded source hashes refer to the original measured source versions; packaged source changes and redactions are documented in `publication-manifest.json`. Historical and unfavorable checkout trials are retained separately from the final comparisons and are not pooled.
