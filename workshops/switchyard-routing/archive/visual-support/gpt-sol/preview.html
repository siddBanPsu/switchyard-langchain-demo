Scope and prototype history

This is a fixed three-stage tool-assisted workflow on one purpose-built synthetic case, repeated three times—not an autonomous-agent benchmark. The earlier agent prototypes had citation, submission and repeated-tool-call problems. They are retained separately in the full findings and are not included in the final comparison.

Routing rules are authored application policy: image or validator-rejected retry → GPT Sol 6.1; routine text → Lightning. Native Switchyard HeaderRoutingConfig performs dispatch. Validators use the labelled fixture for evaluation; this is not a general verifier for arbitrary screenshots.

All original workshop notebooks and backups remain intact.
